# VieriNexus implementation status

## Foundation implemented

- One Dalamud plugin package with separate Domain, Application, Contracts, and Plugin assemblies.
- Polished dark/red/gold application shell based on the supplied visual direction.
- Branded VieriNexus Home experience using the supplied logo; no temporary splash window.
- All Nexus windows remain hidden until a targetable character has settled in the world.
- Mandatory dependency setup gate with six core required providers and a separately labeled recommended-integration catalog audited from every current Vieri product.
- Dependency health detection and focused Install/Enable/Manage actions through Dalamud's plugin installer.
- Nine neutral built-in module registrations, including Routes & Navigation.
- The Progression destination saves the current job's target level, allowed Class/Job/Role-quest, Hunting Log, general-side-quest, and duty methods plus its hard gil reserve. Its primary controls are Start, Stop after this activity, Stop now, and Resume; provider and plan diagnostics are collapsed until requested.
- A pure Reach Job Level planner validates the draft, treats unavailable optional lanes as warnings when another lane is usable, blocks ambiguous or absent providers, and proposes Nexus-owned gear readiness, one exact supported Class/Job/Role or general side quest, one exact Hunting Log target, one bounded duty, and level verification/replanning. Gear, both exact-quest families, open-world Hunting Logs, duty-only Grand Company targets, and ordinary duty steps are executable.
- The Nexus-owned Hunting Log executor selects one exact live target. Open-world work travels through stock Lifestream/vnavmesh, resolves the camp floor, approaches by job range, dismounts, targets the exact monster, runs a temporary Nexus Boss Mod preset, handles repeated kills/alternate camps, and restores prior provider state. Duty-only Grand Company work resolves the exact unlocked dungeon from current game data, requires its stock AutoDuty path, runs it once with AutoDuty leveling disabled, and verifies the exact live kill count after AutoDuty is inactive. Both paths own the appropriate leases and retain Stop ownership until inactivity is confirmed.
- Progress Atlas has one-click bounded actions for the next reachable locked Aetheryte/Aethernet shard, uncollected field Aether Current, currently unlockable quest-earned Aether Current, or unexplored Mapping/Remapping region. Nexus derives quest-current identity and prerequisite readiness from current game data plus the narrow Questionable-compatible status contract, carries a reviewed 152-current coordinate catalog for field targets, and owns exact selection/leases/completion verification/Stop. Quest combat is allowed, completion requires the pinned current/quest, and Stop retains ownership until the quest provider confirms inactivity.
- Capability-checked provider adapters separately detect the VieriCodex and stock Questionable quest contracts plus the VieriAutoDuty or stock AutoDuty duty contract. VieriCodex and stock Questionable loaded together fail closed before either IPC contract is called; after the fork is disabled, stock Questionable becomes the selected quest provider automatically. Nexus uses game-data chapter families and an explicit ordinary-side-quest exclusion policy plus the selected provider's locked/accepted/ready/completed status to choose one exact quest for the current job. The adapter exposes exact single-quest start, current-ID/running observation, completion, and Stop; a quest lacking a provider path is session-blacklisted and skipped under a bounded fallback limit. The selected duty adapter retains exact one-run start, state observation, path eligibility, leveling-mode suppression through either `SetLevelingMode` or stock `SetConfig`, and Stop. Incompatibility detail names the missing boundary.
- Progression panels use content-sized table cells instead of fixed-height no-scroll children, so wrapped descriptions, status messages, buttons, provider details, and plan issues grow under the page's single scrollbar without clipping.
- Character-scoped atomic Progression storage persists desired state, plan revisions, bounded task history, provider/duty/completion checkpoints, and a previous-file recovery copy. The scheduler acquires the full implied duty resource bundle, commands one exact eligible duty with one loop, and never invokes VieriAutoDuty's endless Progression endpoint.
- Dalamud's matching duty-completed event is required in addition to provider start, duty entry, provider shutdown, and return to the world. Wipes, abandonment, provider loss, job changes, and unconfirmed exits block without counting or replaying the run. Stop, provider-loss recovery, reload, and unload retain or reconcile ownership until inactivity is confirmed.
- Stop-after is Nexus policy: it lets the current bounded quest or duty finish and pauses before scheduling another. A normal completion either satisfies the target or creates exactly one newly planned activity from the current permanent level and current eligibility.
- Before each newly selected duty, Nexus creates a separate durable native gear-readiness task. It owns Teleport/Navigation/Movement/UI/Inventory resources, enforces the goal's minimum-gil reserve, runs the exact Nexus approval, and verifies live gil, item level, and a positive completion sequence before releasing ownership or dispatching a duty. Stop, reload, provider loss, unconfirmed completion, gil-floor violation, and item-level regression fail closed.
- Gear & Inventory owns the complete manual and automatic transaction: live equipment/owned-item scan, vendor-band and job-family selection, ordinary gil-shop catalog discovery, role-primary-stat scoring, EXP protection, weakest-ring and two-handed/off-hand policy, exact approval, Nexus/Lifestream/vnavmesh vendor travel, combat-shop menu selection, live-price and reserve rechecks, purchase confirmation, exact per-slot equipping, gearset update, and displaced-item cleanup. It holds Teleport/Navigation/Movement/UI/Inventory until verified completion or confirmed Stop. VieriAutoDuty is no longer used by this module.
- Nexus owns the compact optional Goto/Gear/Inventory/Duty/Extras overlay. It exposes working Nexus route, shopping, configured Progression Start/Resume/Last Run, safe native maintenance, striking-dummy travel, live status, and global Stop controls. Its visibility, position locking, transparent background, and status-line choices are Nexus settings; Nexus will not fall back to stock AutoDuty's overlay.
- A single Nexus-owned control gateway now backs the overlay, `/nexus` chat controls, and versioned trusted-companion IPC. It reports current character/job/location, duty/queue/combat state, gear/inventory metrics, active module/provider/activity, Stop-after, and bounded completion history. Mutation requests require the current character, are capped at 8 KiB, and replay idempotently by request ID. Unsupported VieriAutoDuty-only pause/leave/loop actions fail closed; Nexus never pretends that stock `Stop` is a safe duty leave/resume contract.
- The duty provider inventory now examines every installation claiming the shared `AutoDuty` internal identity. Exactly one loaded instance may satisfy the narrow stock-compatible contract; stock AutoDuty becomes the selected provider automatically when VieriAutoDuty is unloaded, while two loaded instances produce an explicit conflict before any path check, leveling mutation, queue, or Stop call. This closes the ambiguous IPC-registration failure seen during the earlier stock-provider test.
- A transactional VieriAutoDuty operations importer preserves every saved profile, character assignment, retired-item transfer, overlay preference, gear policy, repair/extraction/coffer/desynthesis/Grand Company/Armoire/Glamour/registration/selling rule, thresholds, preferred-vendor payload, and safe in-duty maintenance setting. Import creates a source backup, staged Nexus snapshot, hash receipt, reload verification, and guarded rollback without changing the source or enabling duplicate automation.
- The operations importer accepts explicit JSON `null` and wrong-type optional scalar/array values as safe defaults, rejects malformed non-object profile entries without throwing, and cannot crash startup or the Migration page on a nullable coffer-gearset value.
- Gear & Inventory now owns the protected item-transaction boundary. Desynthesis applies imported category/NQ/skill-up rules while excluding EXP-bonus and gearset equipment. Protected selling requires an exact single-use bag-slot/item/quantity preview, revalidates the entire list and each slot, requires an already-open ordinary NPC shop, and verifies one mutation before touching the next item. Grand Company turn-ins and eligible Armoire/Glamour storage run under Nexus leases, sequencing, Stop, and bounded completion observation. Version 0.1.0.48 makes Nexus own their full prerequisite trip: it selects the character's Grand Company, travels to the exact personnel officer or corresponding inn, approaches the furnishing through vnavmesh, opens it, verifies the eligible item set, and only then calls the narrow AutoRetainer/Glamour Log mechanics IPC. Empty eligible sets are verified no-ops, pre-existing provider work is rejected, and storage completion is rechecked rather than inferred from a busy-state edge. AutoDuty is not called. In-duty withdrawal remains visibly inactive because stock AutoDuty does not expose a safe leave/resume contract.
- Read-only discovery of all nine predecessor configuration sources, including VieriNavPlotter.
- Transactional Routes & Navigation importer with full setting/route mapping, validation preview, timestamped source backup, atomic Nexus-owned staging, SHA-256 receipt, newer-data rollback guard, and restoration of the prior Nexus state. Import never changes the standalone source or activates duplicate navigation.
- The Migration card sizes action buttons from their rendered labels, stacks them when the available width is too small, wraps status/detail text, and scales its panel height with the configured UI scale. A zero-personal-route import explicitly confirms that settings were still imported.

It filters nearby samples, saves each accepted point atomically, refreshes active preview, and stops on territory change, missing route/character, logout, or unload. - No existing Vieri plugin has been disabled, uninstalled, or modified.
